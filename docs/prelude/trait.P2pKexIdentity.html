Expand description

The application’s identity keys and its pins of its peers’ keys. The node never calls it while it holds its own state: a pin may be awaited on storage.

Required Methods§

fn sign(&self, local_cid: u64, body: &[u8]) -> Option<KexAuth>

local_cid’s signature over KEX_TRANSCRIPT_DOMAIN ‖ 0x00 ‖ body, with its identity key; None when the account has no identity key, and its stage goes unsigned.

fn pinned_key(&self, local_cid: u64, peer_cid: u64) -> Option<Vec<u8>>

The identity key local_cid pinned for peer_cid, if any.

fn pin_first_key( &self, local_cid: u64, peer_cid: u64, identity_key: &[u8], ) -> Pin<Box<dyn Future<Output = Result<(), String>> + Send>>

peer_cid signed with a verified key and had no pin: pin it, durably. The exchange goes on, and is reported verified, only once this resolves Ok; an Err refuses it.

fn peer_key_changed(&self, local_cid: u64, peer_cid: u64, presented: &[u8])

peer_cid signed with a verified key other than its pin. The exchange is refused; the pin stays until the person accepts the presented key.
